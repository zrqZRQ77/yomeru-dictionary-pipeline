## JMdict boundary

JMdict-derived forms, readings, senses, and lookup data must remain identifiable as a distinct source layer. Any redistribution must preserve EDRDG/JMdict attribution and the applicable CC BY-SA 4.0 obligations. ShareAlike material must not be silently merged into a separately licensed editorial or commercial layer.

## Engineering disclaimer

This register is an engineering compliance aid, not legal advice. Maintainers must confirm current source terms before distributing a dataset.
